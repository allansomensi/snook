{{GAME}} privacy policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

This policy explains what data the game {{GAME}} and its online server keep, why, who it is shared with, and how you can exercise your rights, including under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Who is responsible

The data controller is {{CONTROLLER}}. Contact: {{CONTACT}}.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data.
  • We show no ads and use no third-party tracking or analytics tools.
  • Card and payment details never pass through the game or the server.

Playing without an account

Games against the computer, two players on one computer and local network games send nothing to the online server. Your settings stay on your computer.

Data kept when you create an account and play online

  • Account: username, country (if you choose one) and your password, stored only as an Argon2 hash (nobody can read it).
  • Recovery e-mail (optional): only if you give one, to recover the account.
  • IP address: used to limit abuse (password guessing, mass sign-ups, message floods). The address used to sign up is stored with the account.
  • Session: an access code for signing in automatically. The server keeps only a hash of it; the code is in the settings file on your computer.
  • Play: games (opponent, result, strokes for replays), rating, statistics, achievements, tournaments, friends and blocks, and the days you played.
  • Chat: lounge and match messages are kept only in the server's memory (the most recent ones) and are not stored. When someone is reported, their latest messages are attached to the report for the moderators.
  • Moderation: reports, bans, mutes and the log of staff actions.
  • Shop: coins, items, coin history and, for real-money purchases made outside the Microsoft Store, the payment record (amount, currency, date) and the e-mail given at checkout.

What we use it for

  • Running online play: accounts, games, ranking, friends, tournaments, the shop.
  • Keeping the game safe and fair: limiting abuse, looking into reports, enforcing the rules.
  • Meeting legal and tax obligations for purchases.

Who we share it with

Only with those who provide services the game needs, and only what they need:

  • Server and database hosting (cloud providers, which may be outside Brazil, e.g. in the United States).
  • Stripe, for real-money purchases made outside the Microsoft Store.
  • Microsoft, when the game is installed or bought through the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's own privacy statement.

Other players see your name, country, rating, statistics, achievements and game history on your profile and in the ranking.

How long

  • For as long as the account exists. Sessions expire after 30 days without use.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data through the contact above. You can also delete your account in the game: Profile → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate: your name, password, country, e-mail and IP address are erased, along with sessions, friends, blocks and achievements. Games already played stay in your opponents' history, linked to an anonymous account, and purchase records are kept without the e-mail for the legal period.

Children

The game has chat between players. Children should play online with a parent or guardian, who can ask for the account to be deleted through the contact above.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated.
